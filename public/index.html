Routing: searoute-ts / Eurostat 2025 MARNET maritime network. Arctic: Northwest/Northeast Passages are blocked by default; enable “Allow Arctic passages” to permit them. No sea-ice or seasonal navigability model is applied. Heading: local route bearing, clockwise from North. Wave data: Copernicus Marine Global Ocean Waves Reanalysis is used for the historical wave series. For missing Arctic wave points, the app checks Copernicus Arctic sea-ice concentration to distinguish ice-affected points from no wave data. This is a research/planning tool, not certified navigation.

Hs — Significant Wave Height: the average height of the highest one-third of individual waves in a wave record. It is commonly used as a measure of the overall sea state.
