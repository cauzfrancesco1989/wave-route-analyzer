VMDR = direction FROM which the waves arrive, clockwise from true North. The rose shows the historical distribution of the individual VMDR observations used for this route point, not a single prevailing-direction arrow. The blue arrow shows the vessel heading and the boat in the centre shows the vessel orientation. This makes it possible to read the wave directions directly relative to the vessel course.
