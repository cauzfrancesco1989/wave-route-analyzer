Routing: searoute-ts / Eurostat 2025 MARNET maritime network. Heading: local route bearing, clockwise from North. Wave data: Copernicus Marine Global Ocean Waves Reanalysis, variable VHM0 (spectral significant wave height), 0.2° grid, 3-hourly. This is a research/planning tool, not certified navigation.

Hs — Significant Wave Height: the average height of the highest one-third of individual waves in a wave record. It is commonly used as a measure of the overall sea state.
